# Deep Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Vehicle Navigation at Unsignalized Intersections

**Authors**: Farhan Tahsin Khan (220041229), Mahmudul Hasan (220041231), A.K.M Ishmam Tahmid (220041259)  
**Department**: Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Islamic University of Technology (IUT)  
**Course**: CSE 4610 / SWE 4606 Design Project  

---

## Abstract
This repository implements an exact-match mathematical reproduction and comparative evaluation of Discrete Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) for autonomous electric vehicle (AEV) navigation at complex, unsignalized 4-way intersections based on the IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (TVT 2024) paper by Xiao et al.
